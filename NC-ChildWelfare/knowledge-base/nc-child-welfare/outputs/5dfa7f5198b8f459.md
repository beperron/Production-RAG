## DSS ADMINISTRATIVE LETTER

## FAMILY SUPPORT AND CHILD WELFARE SERVICES

## FSCWS-10-08

TO: County Directors of Social Services ATTENTION: Children's Services Program Managers and Supervisors

Children's Services Social Workers DATE: May 9, 2008

## SUBJECT: ADDITION TO THE CHILD PLACEMENT AND

## PAYMENT REPORT (DSS-5094)

EFFECTIVE DATE: May 9, 2008

The Division of Social Services and county departments of social services continue to work toward using data more frequently to assist with identifying the areas of greatest need in order to focus resources to serve those children and families with that need.

To support this continued effort, the Child Placement and Payment Report, Form DSS- 5094, was revised to include an additional check box for identifying the number of children coming into foster care as a result of domestic violence. It is located within Field 20, "Reason." If selected, a valid entry of 'X' is to be keyed to the left of "Domestic Violence" or otherwise leave it blank.

Also, the following two reports in NCXPTR will be modified to display domestic violence (DV) and the related totals.

DHRPQA PERM PLAN ANL PQA094-2

DHRPZ PZA084 QUARTERLY CASE LIST

If you have any system questions, please contact DSS Information Support staff at (919) 733-8938. If you have policy questions, please contact Rick Zechman, Special Projects Coordinator at (919) 733-9467.
