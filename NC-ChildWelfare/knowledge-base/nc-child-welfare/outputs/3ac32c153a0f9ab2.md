ATTENTION: CHILDREN'S SERVICES PROGRAM MANAGERS AND SUPERVISORS CHILDREN'S SERVICES SOCIAL WORKERS DATE: February 16, 2010

SUBJECT: REVISED STRUCTURED DECISION MAKING TOOLS EFFECTIVE DATE: March 1, 2010 The statewide implementation date for the revised Structured Decision Making (SDM) Tools is March 1, 2010. The interactive revised tools will be available on the Division of Social Services Forms website on this date. At this time, PDF versions of the documents can be found at the above link and are included in the email concerning this letter. The Division of Social Services held several regional training events supporting implementation of the revised Structured Decision Making Tools. Frequently asked questions from the participants in the work group and training events were accumulated and documented as part of the attached "Question and Answer" document to help provide further guidance and clarification. If you have any questions concerning the implementation of the revised Structured Decision Making Tools, please contact your Children's Program Representative.
