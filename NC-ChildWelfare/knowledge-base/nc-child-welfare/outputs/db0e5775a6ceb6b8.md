## SUBJECT: SOCIAL SECURITY COST- OF-LIVING INCREASE

## FOR 2007 (COLA)

EFFECTIVE DATE: January 1, 2007

EFFECTIVE DATE:

l. GENERAL INFORMATION

The purpose of this letter is to outline procedures for implementing the 2007 Social Security cost-of-living increase for Work First cases only. Effective January 1, 2007, recipients of Social Security (RSDI) and Supplemental Security Income (SSI) will receive a 3.3 % cost-of-living increase (COLA) in the January 2007 payment. Since this will not be a State conducted mass change, counties will be required to add this increase in the income at the household's first change in situation, application or review on or after January 1, 2007.

II. IMPLEMENTATION PROCEDURES

A. Apply this policy to the next review, application, or change in situation on or

after January 1, 2007. Do not key any new SSA/SSI increases prior to January 1, 2007.

B. Verify the new account of the household's SSA/SSI payment using the Online

Verification System (OLV).

<!-- page: 2 -->
C. When the increase in SSA/SSI income is added at the household's first change in situation, application or review, no overpayment will exist for the prior months. If the increased income is not included in that first change in situation, application, or review, any overpayment that occurs in the following months is a county responsible overpayment and should not be charged to the household.
