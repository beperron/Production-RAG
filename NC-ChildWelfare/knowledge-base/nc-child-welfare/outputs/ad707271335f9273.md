DSS ADMINISTRATIVE LETTER FAMLY SUPPORT AND CHILD WELFARE SERVICES FSCWS-12-06 TO: County Directors of Social Services ATTENTION: Children's Services Program Managers and Supervisors Children's Services Social Workers DATE: October 10, 2006

### SUBJECT:

### Changes to Adoption and Foster Care Policy

EFFECTIVE DATE: IMMEDIATELY The purpose of this Administrative Letter is to inform counties of changes to adoption policy resulting from the Deficit Reduction Act of 2006. This change was inadvertently overlooked when Part VI-Adoption Assistance Payments of the Child Welfare Funding, Chapter XIII was sent out on August 7, 2006. Adoption Assistance Eligibility Criteria. The law revises section 473(a)(2) of the Social Security Act to clarify that for title IV-E adoption assistance eligibility, a child must meet the AFDC criteria in the specified relative's home from which he or she is removed. Further, the law simplifies an aspect of adoption assistance eligibility by requiring that a child meet the AFDC eligibility criteria (as they existed in the State's title IV-A plan on July 16, 1996) at the time of the child's removal from a specified relative only. A State is no longer required to determine the child's AFDC eligibility at the time of the initiation of adoption proceedings. For those children who qualify for Adoption Assistance based on receipt of SSI benefits, they must meet both the requirements for SSI benefits and be determined to be a child with special needs prior to the finalization of the adoption. Determination of SSI eligibility is no longer needed at the time the adoption petition is filed. For those children who qualify for Adoption Assistance based on being the child of a minor parent, the child's parent must have received a Title IV-E maintenance payment that covered parent and child and be determined to be a child with special needs prior to the finalization of the adoption. Determination of eligibility is no longer needed at the time the adoption petition is initiated.
