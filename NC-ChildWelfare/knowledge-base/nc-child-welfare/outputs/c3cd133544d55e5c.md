OCTOBER 1, 2010

I. BACKGROUND

The policy on Adoption Vendor Payments is being revised to clarify requirements for approval and to provide a step by step process to utilize in making approvals. The new policy stresses that vendor payments are solely intended to pay for services or treatment that address the adopted child's indentified special needs. They are not intended to supplement the normal and usual costs of raising children.

These revisions to the manuals are being sent to you via e-mail and will only be posted on-line. II. SIGNIFICANT CHANGES

The policy clarifies that approvals must be made annually and provides a step by step process to make decisions on those annual approvals. It also specifies that documentation is not sufficient if it is solely from the provider of the service or treatment. The new policy also clarifies that meeting the educational needs of adopted children is the responsibility of the local education authority and as a result, vendor payment requests for educational needs in general should clearly document that the local educational agency is unable to meet that need.
