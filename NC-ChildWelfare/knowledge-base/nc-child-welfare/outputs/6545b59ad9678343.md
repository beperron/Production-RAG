BACKGROUND | CHANGE INTENT Effective January 1, 2017, safety planning policy regarding Safety Assessments (DSS-5231) and the use of Temporary Parental Safety Agreements shall be used for all Child Protective Services (CPS) Assessments. Temporary Parental Safety Agreements are voluntary, short-term agreements made between a parent and a county child welfare agency when there is a safety threat to a child in his or her own home. The purpose of a Temporary Parental Safety Agreement is to develop a plan with the parent that imposes the lowest level of restriction, disruption, and intrusiveness possible while assuring a child's safety. In partnership with the parent, Temporary Parental Safety Agreements identify safety interventions, who is responsible and within what timeframe, to address each safety indicator identified on the safety assessment. This change notice was initially released December 1, 2016. On December 12, 2016, the policy change was repealed to address concerns related to unintended negative impacts on the court system. This revised change notice removes any reference to time limitations on the use of safety providers. Other aspects of the policy are included in these policy updates. II. SPECIFIC CHANGES

A Temporary Parental Safety Agreement requires the need to separate a child from the home and/or restrict a parent's access to a child, a Temporary Safety Provider should be identified by the parent. To assess the identified Temporary Safety Provider's ability to ensure safety, the Initial Provider Assessment was developed and will replace the existing Initial Kinship Care Assessment. The December 1, 2016, Change Notice also included the requirement for court involvement when a Temporary Safety Provider was still needed to ensure the child's safety at the time of the case decision or during provision of In-Home Services. This December 22, 2016, Change Notice removes from the new policy this requirement for court involvement. With implementation of this new policy, all CPS assessments, family and investigative, will have a timeframe of 45 days for completion.
