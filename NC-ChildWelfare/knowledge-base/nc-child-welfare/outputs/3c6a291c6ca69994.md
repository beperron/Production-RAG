MANUAL: CHILD WELFARE SERVICES MANUAL - CHAPTER VIII SECTION 1426 and 1427 CHANGE NO: 03-2012 TO: COUNTY DIRECTORS OF SOCIAL SERVICES ATTENTION: CHILD WELFARE PROGRAM MANAGERS & SUPERVISORS CHILD WELFARE SOCIAL WORKERS RE: Responsible Individuals List / Central Registry Criminal Code addition EFFECTIVE: June 1, 2012 I. BACKGROUND | CHANGE INTENT Section 1426 and 1427 of the Child Welfare Services Manual provides policy guidance and data entry instruction for the DSS-5104 (Report to the Central Registry) and the DSS-5104a (Responsible Individuals List). Based upon review of N.C.G.S. 7B-324(a)(1), it has become necessary to expand the RIL Indicator Codes to include those situations where the name of the responsible individual is placed on the RIL as the result of a criminal conviction of the same incident that led to the involvement of the local DSS. II. SPECIFIC CHANGES Although uncommon, cases have occurred where the individual is criminally convicted prior to the DSS case decision being made. The following codes have been developed for entry on the DSS- 5104a. When selected these codes will automatically populate to the central registry to correctly identify the method by which a person's name is placed on the RIL.

|Code|Value|RIL Placement?|
|---|---|---|
|1C|Abuse|Yes|
|2C|Serious Neglect|Yes|
|3C|Abuse and Serious Neglect|Yes|
|4C|Abuse and/or Neglect|Yes|
|5C|Serious Neglect and Neglect|Yes|
