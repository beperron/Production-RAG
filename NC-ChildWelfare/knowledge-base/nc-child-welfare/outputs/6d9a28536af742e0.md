DATE: MARCH 11, 2013 MANUAL: CHILD WELFARE SERVICES - FOSTER HOME LICENSING TO: COUNTY DIRECTORS OF SOCIAL SERVICES AND DIRECTORS OF CHILD PLACING AGENCIES ATTENTION: CHILD WELFARE PROGRAM ADMINISTRATORS, MANAGERS, SUPERVISORS, AND WORKERS; ALL FOSTER HOME LICENSING WORKERS RE: AMMENDMENTS TO THE FOSTER HOME LICENSING MANUAL TO INCLUDE: TABLE OF CONTENTS, SECTION .0700, SECTION .1100, CHAPTER VII, AND CHAPTER IX EFFECTIVE: IMMEDIATELY

I. BACKGROUND | CHANGE INTENT

The Foster Home Licensing Manual was last issued July 1, 2012 (http://info.dhhs.state.nc.us/olm/manuals/dss/csm-94/man/). Revisions have been made in several sections of the manual to use consistent language when referring to requirements for individuals 18 years of age and older. Clarifications have been made to policies related to required applicants, weekly supervision for therapeutic foster parents, exterior setting and safety, licensing visits and reporting juvenile convictions. Revisions have been made to update General Statute 131D-10.6C and information related to household members who enter the United States on a VISA. The manual replaces guidance for licensing foster homes that has been provided in memos, webinars, Supplemental Guide to Foster Home Licensing and foster home licensing training.

III. INSTRUCTIONS

Please note the above changes to the Foster Home Licensing Manual (http://info.dhhs.state.nc.us/olm/manuals/dss/csm-94/man/) and begin using the revised manual immediately.

Please note that the Foster Home Licensing Manual is available on-line as PDF documents only. Previously, the North Carolina Division of Social Services has maintained both "on-line" versions of its manuals along with PDF versions. Beginning with this manual, only the PDF versions will be available at: http://info.dhhs.state.nc.us/olm/manuals/manuals.aspx?dc=dss. Navigation between sections of this manual will require users to use the "Back" button of their browser to return to the Table of Contents for this manual to select the next session to be read.

Questions related to the Foster Home Licensing Manual should be directed to the Licensing Authority Program Consultants at 828-669-3388.
