### CHANGE NOTICE FOR MANUAL 04-2007

DATE: January 11, 2007 MANUAL: Family Services Services Manual, Volume 1: Children's Services, Chapter VIII: Child Protective Services Section 1411, Safe Surrender TO: County Directors of Social Services ATTENTION: CPS Program Managers and Supervisors

CPS Social Workers RE: Safe Surrender Policy EFFECTIVE: Immediately

Administrative Letter #C.S. 2-01 dated October 2, 2001 provided some guidance regarding the Infant Homicide Prevention Act, commonly referred to as Safe Surrender, that became effective on 7-19-2001. Since that time the Division, in collaborated with the NCACDSS, decided that a more comprehensive policy was needed. Section 1411 has been added to Chapter VIII to provide more guidance on Safe Surrender. This policy also includes instruction for entering data into the Central Registry.

Should you have any questions regarding the policy please contact your Children's Program Representative or the Work First/CPS Policy Team at (919) 733-4622. For questions concerning Central Registry Data Entry, please contact Kate Johnson at (919) 733-3801 or katherine.johnson@ncmail.net.
