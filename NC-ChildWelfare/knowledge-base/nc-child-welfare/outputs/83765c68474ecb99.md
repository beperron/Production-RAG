# Request for

# Review by

# Prosecutor or

# DHHS

If NC DHHS has been requested to conduct a review, the County Director shall be notified of time/place for review. The local agency must immediately transmit a copy of the summary of the assessment.

The reviewing party must complete the review within 20 days of receipt and include conferences with the reporter, the CPS social worker, the juvenile (if possible) and any other people known to have pertinent information about the juvenile and family.

NC DHHS may also direct the County Director to take specific action to provide protective services.

Unless the reporter waives notification, within 5 working days of case decision, the County Director shall give subsequent written notice to reporter of the findings, whether the county is taking action to protect, & what action is taken—to include if a petition is filed.

If a petition is not filed, the reporter must request a review of the case by the prosecutor or NC DHHS within 5 working days of receiving the second notification. Nothing precludes the reporter from requesting a review from both the prosecutor and NC DHHS.

NC DHHS must notify the Prosecutor within 2 business days of receiving a request for review. The Prosecutor may decide to conduct a review at this time.
