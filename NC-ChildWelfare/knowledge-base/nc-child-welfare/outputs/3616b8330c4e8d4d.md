### CHANGE NOTICE

CWS-CN-06-21 MANUAL: APPENDIX 3.5 CHILD WELFARE FUNDING FOSTER CARE & APPENDIX 2 - CPPS MANUAL CHANGE NO: CWS-CN-06-21 ATTENTION: CHILD WELFARE DIRECTORS, PROGRAM ADMINISTRATORS, MANAGERS, SUPERVISORS, AND SOCIAL WORKERS RE: REVISIONS TO APPENDIX 3.5 AND INSTRUCTIONS FOR DSS-5094, FIELD 55 EFFECTIVE: IMMEDIATELY INTENT: NOTIFICATION OF APPENDIX 3.5 AND CPPS UPDATES Appendix 3.5 Child Welfare Funding Manual has been revised and updated to incorporate the following required changes in response the Family First Prevention Services Act and federal IV-E regulations:

• Clarify and accurately reflect when administrative costs can be claimed for a IV-E eligible child. • Addition of the 14-day limit on claiming for maintenance payments for most new group placements made after September 1, 2021. • Clarification of the differences in maintenance and administrative claiming for children under 18 and young adults 18-21. • New foster care board rates effective January 1, 2022.
