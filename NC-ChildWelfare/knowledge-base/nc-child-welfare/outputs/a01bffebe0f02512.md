I. BACKGROUND | CHANGE INTENT

The Foster Home Licensing Manual was issued July 1, 2012 (http://info.dhhs.state.nc.us/olm/manuals/dss/csm-94/man/). Revisions have been made to clarify guidance related to Quarterly Visits by Foster Home Licensing Social Workers and Citizenship Status of Foster Parent Applicants. References to the Supplemental Guide to Foster Home Licensing have been deleted.

II. SPECIFIC CHANGES

A. Table of Contents

□ Chapter VIII Miscellaneous, Item F Supplemental Guide to Foster Home Licensing is deleted. □ Chapter VIII Miscellaneous, Item I Citizenship Status of Foster Parents is added.

B. VII Section .1100

C. VIII Miscellaneous

CHANGE NOTICE FOR MANUAL

Please note the above changes to the Foster Home Licensing Manual (http://info.dhhs.state.nc.us/olm/manuals/dss/csm-94/man/) and begin using the revised manual immediately.
