DATE: APRIL 5, 2013 MANUAL: CHILD WELFARE MANUAL. CHAPTER VIII. 1422. CHILD MEDICAL & CHILD/FAMILY EVALUATION PROGRAM. TO: COUNTY DIRECTORS OF CHILD WELFARE AGENCIES. ATTENTION: CHILD WELFARE PROGRAM DIRECTORS, CHILD WELFARE SUPERVISORS & CHILD WELFARE WORKERS. RE: REVISION OF CHAPTER VIII. 1422. CHILD MEDICAL & CHILD/FAMILY EVALUATION PROGRAM (CMEP/CFEP). EFFECTIVE: APRIL 30, 2013

I. BACKGROUND | CHANGE INTENT

The Child Medical and Child/Family Evaluation Program (Section 1422) was last updated in January 2007. Revisions have been made in areas of the section to use consistent language when referring to public local child welfare agencies. Clarifications have been made to policies related to CME and CFE Programs.

II. SPECIFIC CHANGES

 Consistent language is used throughout the policy to identify public agencies that are required to provide protective services for children. "Department of Social Services" had been used in the past. That has now been updated as "County Child Welfare Agency".  Clarification on when a referral to the CMEP/CFEP is appropriate.  Language and acronym changes for the CFEP (it is no longer referred to as the Child Mental Health Evaluation Program)  Updated FAX number for the CMEP/CFEP Office.

III. INSTRUCTIONS

Please note the above changes to the Child Medical & Child/Family Evaluation Program and begin using the revised manual immediately.

Questions related to the Child Medical & Child/Family Evaluation Program should be directed to the Child Medical & Child/Family Evaluation Program Office at 919 843 9365.
