DSS-5025 North Carolina Evidence Based Services Plan: Completion Instructions

When to complete the North Carolina Evidence Based Services Plan: The North Carolina Evidence Based Services Plan (DSS-5025) is only developed when a new case assigned to CPS In-Home Services, after substantiation or a "services needed" finding is made, AND the In-Home Family Case Plan (DSS-5239) has not yet been developed, AND one or more of the child(ren) is/are at imminent risk of removal if services are not promptly provided to prevent county child welfare agency custody AND The child is eligible to receive an Evidence-Based Prevention (EBP) service included in North Carolina's Title IV-E Prevention Services Plan. (see https://www.ncdhhs.gov/divisions/socialservices/child-welfare-services/family-first-prevention-services-act) Case documentation should reflect diligent efforts to engage the family in a discussion about available services and their interest in participating in those services. Purpose of the Evidence Based Services Plan: This temporary agreement is developed in collaboration with a family only when services are needed immediately to safely prevent the child from needing to enter foster care and before the DSS-5239 In Home Family Case Plan can be completed. The plan must clearly outline:

 concerning parental behaviors,  the impact on child safety,  and an agreed upon EBP service that will address the identified risk. Parent/Caretaker signatures indicate their acceptance of services and participation in plan development. If a caretaker is unwilling or unable to provide a signature but gives verbal consent, the In-Home caseworker must document

 the caregiver's willingness to participate in the service,  reason for the parent's non-participation or refusal to sign,  and the date they agreed to the service. Elements of the Evidence Based Services Plan must be incorporated in the development of the In-Home Family Case Plan (DSS-5239). All subsequent EBP services planning must be documented on the In-Home Family Case Plan (DSS-5239). Development of Evidence Based Services Plan: The North Carolina Evidence Based Services Plan form is developed with the family by the CPS In-Home Services caseworker or other worker as assigned. The plan must be developed with the family members who are agreeing to engage in an Evidence-Based Prevention service. In domestic violence cases, separate plans should be completed with the non-offending parent/adult victim and the perpetrator of domestic violence. The perpetrator of domestic violence should not have access to the non-offending parent/adult victim's plan. A Child and Family Team meeting is not required. Complete Identifying Information: County: name of the North Carolina county responsible for the delivery of services Family Name: case identifier

DSS-5025 North Carolina Evidence Based Services Plan: Completion Instructions

Case #: county case number Caseworker: name of the caseworker completing the form Supervisor: name of the supervisor approving the form Date Plan Developed: date the plan was developed with the family Risk of Entry into Foster Care and Prevention Service(s): Child Name: name of child being assessed Date of Birth: date of birth of child being assessed: MM/DD/YYYY CNDS ID Number: Common Name Data Service ID, or the child's Medicaid ID, is found in PATH NC under the participant tab. If not already assigned, one will need to be generated. Service to address risk: The identified Evidence-Based Prevention service(s) the family has agreed to work with. Service participant(s): The family members that have agreed to participate in the service. Service provider: The agency / provider that will be working with the family. Is the child at serious or imminent risk of entry into foster care?: Check "Yes" or "No" to document whether each child is at serious or imminent risk of entry into foster care if effective preventive services are not provided. Depending on the circumstances in the household, the responses may or may not be the same for each child. If "Yes," provide clear and concise language regarding the specific reason that the child(ren) is/are at imminent risk of removal if services are not promptly provided: Describe the caretakers behavior or lack of action and its impact on the child(rens) safety. Signatures: Ensure you are capturing the names and signatures of all participants, their role in the family and the date they signed the plan. If a caretaker is unwilling or unable to provide a signature but gives verbal consent, the In-Home caseworker must document how they participated, their willingness to engage in services and the date. Caseworker completing the form should sign the document and provide a copy to the family. The supervisor should review the documentation and sign it. This form must be uploaded to PATH NC upon completion.
