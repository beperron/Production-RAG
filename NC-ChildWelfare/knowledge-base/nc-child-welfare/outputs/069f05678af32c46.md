I. GENERAL INFORMATION

The purpose of this letter is to outline procedures for implementing the 2004 Social Security cost-of-living increase for Work First cases only. Effective January 1, 2005, recipients of Social Security (RSDI) and Supplemental Security Income (SSI) will receive a 2.7% cost-of-living increase (COLA). In the past, these increases were budgeted in Work First cases via a State implemented

II. IMPLEMENTATION PROCEDURES

A. Apply this policy to the next review, application, or change in situation on or

after January 1, 2005. Do not key any new SSA/SSI increases prior to

January 1, 2005.

B. Verify the new amount of the household's SSA/SSI payments in SDX/

BENDEX.

C. When the increase in SSA/SSI income is added at the household's first

change in situation, application or review, no overpayment will exist for the prior months. If the increased income is not included in that first change in situation, application, or review, any overpayment that occurs in the following months is a county responsible overpayment and should not be charged to the household.

If you have any questions regarding this information, please contact your Work First Representative.
