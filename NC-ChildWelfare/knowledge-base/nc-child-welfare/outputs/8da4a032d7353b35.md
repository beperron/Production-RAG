NORTH CAROLINA COURT OF APPEALS RULING - CONSTITUTIONALITY OF THE RESPONSIBLE INDIVIDUALS LIST EFFECTIVE:

RE:

IMMEDIATELY I. BACKGROUND / CHANGE INTENT

In May of 2006, the Division implemented the Responsible Individuals List as a result of legislation that was passed directing the Division to establish policies and procedures for individuals determined to have abused or seriously neglect a juvenile, to request expunction from a list that was available to certain employers. On March 2, 2010, the North Carolina Court of Appeals issued an opinion (http://www.aoc.state.nc.us/www/public/coa/opinions/2010/pdf/090205-1.pdf) that the Responsible Individuals List, as currently administered, is unconstitutional. II. SIGNIFICANT CHANGES

The Division is taking measures to comply with this ruling. Family Services Manual (Volume 1); Chapter VIII; Section 1427 (Expunction) and all of that section's attachments are now obsolete. This section is no longer available on the Division's manuals website http://info.dhhs.state.nc.us/olm/manuals/manuals.aspx?dc=dss). Please remove this section from any hardcopy manuals in your agency. Additional information regarding the impact of the Court of Appeals ruling will be forthcoming. Should you have further questions please contact your agency's assigned Children's Program Representative or the Child Welfare Policy team at (919) 733-4622.
