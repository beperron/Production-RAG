ATTENTION: CHILD WELFARE DIRECTORS, PROGRAM ADMINISTRATORS, MANAGERS, SUPERVISORS, AND SOCIAL WORKERS

RE: DSS-5104 A CLARIFICATION TO INSTRUCTIONS FIELD 12

EFFECTIVE DATE: IMMEDIATELY

INTENT:

The DSS- 5104 A Responsible Individuals List Form, Field 12 Date of Notice to Responsible Individual Delivered instructions have been revised and policy updated.

Documenting the date, the alleged perpetrator received their notice about the placement of their name on the Responsible Individuals list, is captured in Field 12. This field also documents when the responsible individual is unable to be served. In this instance, in lieu of notice delivery, an ex parte order is entered. This field now reads:

Field 12 - Date Notice delivered to alleged perpetrator/ ex parte order entered. Enter the date (in the eight-digit numeric MMDDYYYY format) that the notice to the responsible individual was delivered. This date cannot precede the Date of Case Decision listed in Field 10 on the DSS-5104. If the responsible individual is unable to be located, enter the date ex parte order entered. This change has been made in Appendix 1: CPS Data Collection, Section VI. DSS-5104a Form Completion: Fields and Codes.
