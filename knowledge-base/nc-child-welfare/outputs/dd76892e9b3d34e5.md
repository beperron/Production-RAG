DATE: JUNE 15, 2013 MANUAL: CHILD WELFARE SERVICES MANUAL. TIME-LIMITED FAMILY REUNIFICATION SERVICES TO: DIRECTORS OF COUNTY CHILD WELFARE AGENCIES ATTENTION: CHILD WELFARE STAFF AND ATTORNEYS RE: REVISION OF TIME-LIMITED FAMILY REUNIFICATION SERVICES POLICY EFFECTIVE: JULY 1, 2013

I. BACKGROUND | CHANGE INTENT

Policy for the Intensive Family Reunification Services program was last updated in March 2009. Revisions have been made to reflect several changes needed as a result of the shift from private providers to the local public Child Welfare agencies. Prior to June 30, 2013, these services have been provided by private agencies. As of July 1, 2013, funding will be distributed directly to all public child welfare agencies. Client eligibility and allowable activities will be determined by Federal requirements.

II. SPECIFIC CHANGES

 The program name will be known as "Time-Limited Family Reunification Services".  The following sections of policy have been removed:

o II. D. Key Elements of Design; o III. C. Referral Sources; o III. E. Reunification Forms; o III. F. Staff Qualifications; o III. G. Training; o III. H. Contractor Responsibilities and o IV. Program Reporting, Evaluating and Monitoring.  Revisions have been made for record-keeping and documentation.

III. INSTRUCTIONS

Please note the above changes to Time-Limited Family Reunification Services program and begin using the revised manual as of July 1, 2013.
