DATE: MARCH 1, 2013 MANUAL: CHILD WELFARE SERVICES MANUAL - CHAPTER XI INTERSTATE/INTERCOUNTRY SERVICES TO CHILDREN TO: COUNTY DIRECTORS OF SOCIAL SERVICES | HUMAN SERVICES AGENCIES ATTENTION: CHILD WELFARE STAFF RE: AMENDMENTS TO INTERSTATE COMPACT ON THE PACEMENT OF CHILDREN (ICPC) REGULATIONS: II, III, IV, V, VII AND ADOPTION OF REGULATION XII EFFECTIVE: IMMEDIATELY

I. BACKGROUND | CHANGE INTENT

The Association of Administrators of the Interstate Compact on the Placement of Children (ICPC) amended Regulations II, III, and VII, to provide clarity and accountability in ensuring that placements made through ICPC are safe, timely, and expedited when appropriate. These amendments were incorporated into the ICPC and effective October 1, 2011. To promote better collaboration and increase accountability, The Association of Administrators amended Regulations IV and V. Regulation XII was adopted to provide guidance on processing Private and Independent Adoptions.

II. SPECIFIC CHANGES

A. Regulation II allows a reconsideration of a denial of and requires a signed Regulation II Case Manager Statement of Interest (DSS-1841) found at http://info.dhhs.state.nc.us/olm/forms/dss/dss-1841-ia.pdf to be included in all Regulation II requests. B. Regulation III has been amended to provide more definitions, including placement categories, applicability and exemptions under the Compact. This section also provides clarification of commonly used terms in ICPC. C. The amended Regulation IV clarifies the role of the Psychiatric Residential Treatment Facility (PRTF) monitoring the child while he/she is in the receiving state. The regulation also addresses the abandonment of children in the receiving state, the time frames for processing of PRTF requests, and establishes the documents necessary to make a referral. D. Regulation V clarifies the role of Interstate Compact on the Placement of Children liaisons for those states that have decentralized some responsibilities for ICPC. E. Regulation VII was previously termed "Priority Placement" and was revised to utilize the term "Expedited Placement" decision. Regulation VII also requires a Regulation VII Case Manager Statement of Interest (DSS-1840) found at http://info.dhhs.state.nc.us/olm/forms/dss/dss-1840-ia.pdf indicating the interest of the placement resource and that s/he is willing to cooperate in the ICPC process. An Order of Compliance (DSS- 1839) found at http://info.dhhs.state.nc.us/olm/forms/dss/dss-1839-ia.pdf is now required to be included in all Regulation VII requests and must be signed by a judge.

F. The ICPC Checklist (DSS-5255) found at http://info.dhhs.state.nc.us/olm/forms/dss/dss-5255-ia.pdf has been revised to assist in providing guidance on developing an appropriate referral. G. Regulation XII is a new regulation added to provide guidance and requirements for the processing of Independent and Private Agency adoptions across state lines. The purpose of the regulation is to ensure protection and service to children and families involved in executing adoptions across state lines.
