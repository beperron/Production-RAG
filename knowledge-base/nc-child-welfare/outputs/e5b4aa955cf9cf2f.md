TO: COUNTY DIRECTORS OF SOCIAL SERVICES ATTENTION: ALL CHILD WELFARE STAFF DATE: APRIL 15, 2016

SUBJECT: NEW HEALTH HISTORY AND HEALTH SUMMARY FORMS TO BE COMPLETED FOR CHILDREN AND YOUTH IN FOSTER CARE EFFECTIVE: APRIL 15, 2016 I. Intent and Background The purpose of this Administrative Letter is to provide information and instructions regarding new health forms to be completed for children and youth in foster care. The development of these forms was primarily driven by Fostering Health NC, an initiative led by the NC Pediatric Society, and were designed to document current critical health information about children and youth and to more closely support the American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP)-recommended initial visit, 30-day comprehensive visit, and recurring well-visits for children and youth entering foster care. The new health forms can be found on the NC DHHS, DSS publications website at http://info.dhhs.state.nc.us/. The NC Pediatric Society also funded the development of 2 self-paced, on-demand, online trainings available on http://www.ncswlearn.org: Fostering Connections I: Partnering to Improve the Health and Well-being of Children in Foster Care and Fostering Connections II: Building Local Systems to Improve the Health and Well-being of Children in Foster Care. These trainings include guidance on using the forms introduced in this communication. They are available to anyone with an ncswLearn.org account. Fostering Connections I is recommended for agency directors, child welfare workers, supervisors, and managers working in the areas of child placement, LINKS, licensing, and adoption. Fostering Connections II is recommended for agency directors, child welfare supervisors, program managers, and program administrators working in the areas of child placement, LINKS, licensing, and adoption.

The following forms and instructions replace forms DSS-5243 Child Health Status Component, and the DSS-5244 Child Physical Examination form. These forms are now obsolete and have been removed from the NC DHHS, DSS publications website. II.
