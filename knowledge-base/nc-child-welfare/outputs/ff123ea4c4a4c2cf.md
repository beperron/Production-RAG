POLICY RESULTING FROM FOSTERING CONNECTIONS TO SUCCESS AND INCREASING ADOPTIONS ACT OF 2008; EDUCATION STABILITY FOR CHILDREN IN FOSTER CARE EFFECTIVE:

RE:

IMMEDIATELY I. BACKGROUND / CHANGE INTENT

On October 7, 2008, the Fostering Connections to Success and Increasing Adoptions Act of 2008 (Public Law 110-351) was signed into law. DSS Administrative Letter CWS- AL-02-09 dated March 17, 2009 provides detailed information on the impact of this federal legislation to North Carolina.. This policy update and form (underlined and in red) brings North Carolina into compliance with one of the provisions outlined in the Fostering Connections to Success and Increasing Adoptions Act.

II. SIGNIFICANT CHANGES

Family Services Manual; Volume I; Chapter IV; Section 1201; Out of Home Placement Services provides policy and guidance on services required for children placed into foster care. Specific services must be fulfilled within the first two weeks of placement, including planning for the children's educational needs. This policy update addresses how educational instability can cause harm to children in foster care and amends the Child Education Status Component (DSS-5245) form to include case planning requirements per the federal act.
