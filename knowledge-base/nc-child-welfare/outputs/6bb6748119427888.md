### CHANGE NOTICE FOR MANUAL

DATE: July 31, 2008 MANUAL: Family Services Manual Volume 1: Children's Services, Chapter VIII CHANGE NO: 06-2008 TO: County Directors of Social Services ATTENTION: CPS Program Managers and Supervisors CPS Social Workers RE: Section 1428, Confidentiality and Release of Information EFFECTIVE: Immediately

BACKGROUND

This policy was originally developed to ensure that there were stringent confidentiality rules regarding the release of any information that may have been obtained during a CPS assessment. Families have a right to privacy and the child has a right to protection from public disclosure of that information.

SPECIFIC CHANGES This updated policy clarifies 42 CFR Part 2 concerning the disclosure and re-disclosure of confidential alcohol and drug abuse patient records and under what circumstances these records can be disclosed. There have been definitions added to help clarify what information in the record would be covered by 42 CFR Part 2. There has also been an update concerning the Adam Walsh legislation and release of information from the Central Registry. System of Care language has been added. The majority of the changes are not policy, but grammatical to clarify the policy and structure to make it easier to read. A Consent for Release of Confidential Information tool, DSS-5297, has been developed that is Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) and 42 CFR Part 2 compliant.
