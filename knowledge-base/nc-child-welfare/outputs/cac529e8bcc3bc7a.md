### CHANGE NOTICE FOR MANUAL

DATE: November 22, 2006 MANUAL: Family Services Services Manual, Volume 1: Children's Services, Chapter VIII: Child Protective Services, Section 1409, Domestic Violence CHANGE NO: 03-2006 TO: County Directors of Social Services ATTENTION: CPS Supervisors and CPS Social Workers RE: Revised Manual and Forms EFFECTIVE: Immediately In early 2004, the Division of Social Services collaborated with the NCACDSS Workgroup to develop the Domestic Violence Policy. The policy was released in September 2004. After hearing from training participants in the "Child Welfare Domestic Violence Policy Training," policy guidance on conducting Child and Family Team meetings with families experiencing domestic violence has been revised. These policy revisions have been approved by the NCACDSS. Revised policy provides updated guidance for using Child and Family Team (CFT) meetings in CPS cases involving domestic violence. In some cases, the non-offending parent/adult victim may want the perpetrator of domestic violence to participate in the Child and Family Team meeting together. The non-offending parent/adult victim may see this as a safer opportunity to negotiate agreements in regards to the care of the children as well as a way to bring community pressure on the perpetrator to change their behaviors and stop the violence. This revised policy allows for the joint CFT meetings in certain situations. The revised policy provides several discussion questions to help guide the decision as to whether to go forward with a joint Child and Family Team meeting. Also, if the decision by the County DSS and/or facilitator is to go forward with a joint Child and Family Team meeting, the revised policy provides some examples of safety preparations that may be needed to ensure the safety of the meeting participants before, during and after the meeting. The Kinship Care Initial Assessment (DSS-5203) and Instructions (DSS-5204) have been revised to include a question about a caregiver's history of domestic violence. The Family Assessment of Strengths and Needs (DSS-5229), S5. Family Relationships, has clarified the definition of c. "domestic discord" and d. "serious domestic discord/domestic violence" to be more consistent with the current definition of domestic violence in policy.
