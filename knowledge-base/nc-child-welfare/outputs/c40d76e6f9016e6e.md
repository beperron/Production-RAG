### CHANGE NOTICE FOR MANUAL

DATE: MARCH 1, 2017 MANUAL: Chapter XIII: Child Welfare Funding Manual CHANGE NO: CWS-CN-03-2017 TO: COUNTY DIRECTORS OF SOCIAL SERVICES / HUMAN SERVICES ATTENTION: CHILD WELFARE PROGRAM MANAGERS & SUPERVISORS CHILD WELFARE SOCIAL WORKERS RE: Revision to Chapter XIII: Child Welfare Funding Manual, Section 1700 - Guardianship Assistance Program. EFFECTIVE: Immediately

I. BACKGROUND | CHANGE INTENT

The Child Welfare Funding Manual has been updated to incorporate the changes needed for the approval and implementation of the State's IV-E Plan, to clarify certain areas of policy; including circumstances under which Guardianship Assistance benefits are terminated, and other language and formatting updates. Revisions made to the policy are in red underlined font.

II. SPECIFIC CHANGES

The changes include: 

I. Purpose and Legal Basis - Addition of language related to Fostering Connections to

Success and Increasing Adoptions Act of 2008 as follows: o This Act also requires states who have implemented a guardianship assistance program to exercise due diligence in identifying and providing notice to adult relatives and all parents having custody of siblings of their options for participating in the care and placement of the child, and the process and requirements for subsequently entering into a guardianship assistance agreement.  II. Definitions - Clarification of Guardianship Assistance definition.  III. Eligibility - Addition of the following: o Eligibility criteria a. The child has been removed from his or her home pursuant to a voluntary placement agreement or as a result of a judicial determination to the effect that continuation in the home would be contrary to the welfare of the child. o Revision of eligibility criteria, formerly letter b., now letter c. to clarify that a child is eligible for guardianship assistance benefits if the child is eligible for foster care maintenance payments and has been placed in a licensed home of the prospective legal guardian for a minimum of 6 consecutive months. o Revision of eligibility criteria, formerly letter d., now letter e. to include criteria for sibling eligibility that the prospective legal guardian and the county child welfare agency agree on the appropriateness of the arrangement for the sibling. o The addition of an option for continuation of benefits to age 21 if warranted due to some physical or mental disability with the state's approval. V. Documentation and Periodic Reviews - Addition of more specific language regarding the

responsibility of the legal guardian(s) to notify the county child welfare agency of changes related to their legal responsibility or financial support for the child. Addition of language regarding the requirement that counties must ensure that children who receive guardianship assistance benefits and are compulsory school age are enrolled full-time in elementary or secondary school or have completed secondary school. If the child is not enrolled as a fulltime student, documentation must support that the child is unable to attend on a full-time basis due to a medical, physical, or therapeutic condition.  Addition of X. Termination of Guardianship Assistance Benefits/Payments - This section describes the specific circumstances under which guardianship assistance benefits will be terminated.
