ALL CHILD WELFARE STAFF DATA ENTRY STAFF RE: IMPLEMENTATION OF THE AUTOMATED RESPONSIBLE INDIVIDUALS LIST (RIL) EFFECTIVE: OCTOBER 17, 2011 I. BACKGROUND / CHANGE INTENT

On March 2, 2010, the North Carolina Court of Appeals issued an opinion finding that the statutes governing the procedures surrounding the RIL violated the North Carolina Constitution (http://www.aoc.state.nc.us/www/public/coa/opinions/2010/pdf/090205-1.pdf). In response, new legislation was ratified on July 8, 2010. This new legislative authority for the RIL can be found in G.S. §§ 7B-101, 7B-311(d), 7B-320, and 7B-323.

Policy revisions were issued in August of 2010 regarding the RIL are located in the Child Welfare Services Manual; Chapter VIII; Section 1427. The RIL impacts other sections of child welfare policy, including policy on the use and entry of information into the Central Registry (Child Welfare Services Manual; Chapter VIII; Section 1426). The purpose of this notice is to highlight interpretation and automation changes in the impacted policy sections. Incorporated into policy are the changes outlined in Administrative Letter CWS-AL-05-11.

The Division is pleased to announce that effective October 17, 2011 information captured on the DSS-5104a regarding the placement of an individuals name on the RIL will be automated. It will once again be the responsibility of county departments of Social Services to enter information into the state network automated system. The relevant policies and forms will be placed on-line October 17, 2011. II. SIGNIFICANT CHANGES

• Section 1426 o The policy and the Users manual are combined into one section. o Language and instructions are clarified regarding data entry of information is as it relates to Service Information System (SIS) and RIL. o The DSS-5104d has been updated o Field 23- Perpetrator Relationship Code now includes information on documenting cases where allegations are substantiated but there is no known perpetrator. Code "00" has been added to reflect that although the child may be abused or neglected, the identity of the perpetrator is unknown. This code is to be used when abuse (1), neglect (2), abuse and neglect (3), abuse and serious neglect (3S), serious neglect (2S), or neglect and serious neglect (2N) has been substantiated but the specific perpetrator cannot be identified. Use of this code now requires an entry of "05" in Field 31.

o Field 39- RIL Indicator Status. The code in this field is automatically "00" until a DSS-5104a is completed. The RIL indicator will automatically be updated based on the information listed on the RIL. • Section 1427 Guidance is added to clarify that the use of the RIL is not optional when substantiating a case for abuse or serious neglect. o Guidance surrounding the delivery of the personal written notice now includes instructions that the alleged responsible individual should sign an acknowledgment that they have received the case decision notice. In the event that the notice of placement on the RIL is sent certified return receipt requested because it could not delivered in person and the notice be returned as undeliverable or signed for by someone other than the alleged responsible individual, the name shall not be placed on the RIL. o In the event that there is a lack of identifying information for the alleged responsible individual, such as a date of birth or social security number, but the individual did receive the notice it is permissible to place the name on the RIL provided that there is at least one identifier. However, if the notice is undeliverable and there is no identifying information, the name shall not go on the RIL. o Clarification regarding the agency's authority to continue working with a family is provided for situations in which a Judicial Review determines that the alleged responsible individual's name should not be placed on the RIL. o Instructions for the completion of the DSS-5104a are updated and additional codes have been created to accommodate the automation system. o The DSS-5104d is revised to reflect a deletion request related to the RIL.
