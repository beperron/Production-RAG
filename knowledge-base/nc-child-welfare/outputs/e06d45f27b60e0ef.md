### CHANGE NOTICE FOR MANUAL

DATE: February 28, 2008 MANUAL: Family Services Manual, Volume 1: Children's Services, Chapter VIII: Section 1408 CHANGE NO: 03-2008

TO: County Directors of Social Services ATTENTION: CPS Program Managers and Supervisors CPS Social Workers RE: Clarifications and updates to current policy EFFECTIVE: April 1, 2008

Chapter VIII, Section 1408 Investigative and Family Assessments, has been updated with the following changes. Language from the revision of Chapter V, Jurisdiction in Child Welfare, has been incorporated through out the section. Clarification regarding the safe surrender policy located in section 1411 has been incorporated through out the section. The model format for case decision letter to the reporter in family and investigative assessments has been revised and combined into one new letter. The one change to policy is located in section II, H., (3) and is regarding the CAPTA requirement to refer children to Early Intervention Services. This is in response to clarification from the federal government. It is no longer required that every child under the age of three who has been the subject of a substantiated abuse, neglect or dependency case be referred to Early Intervention. It is now permissible for county Departments of Social Services to screen this population and only refer those who have an identified need. To screen a child for referral to Early Intervention, counties are directed to use the current Strength and Needs Assessment (DSS-5229), item S6. Child Characteristics. Any identification of need on this item will require that the referral to the CDSA be made. Additional instructions on the DSS-5229 for completing item S6. have been included. It is important to remember that at any time there is a concern regarding the developmental state of a child, that a referral be made. Once it has been determined that a referral to Early Intervention has been made, it is not necessary to make subsequent referrals when new reports are received.
