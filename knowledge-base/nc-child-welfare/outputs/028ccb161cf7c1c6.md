### Purpose of the Plan

This plan is being developed because one or more children in the family are at imminent or serious risk of entering foster care absent effective preventive services. The plan describes the risk(s) to each child and identifies Evidence Based Prevention service(s) intended to safely prevent the child from needing to enter foster care.
