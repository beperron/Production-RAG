CHANGE NOTICE FOR MANUAL

DATE: January 31, 2014 MANUAL: Child Placement and Payment System Manual Instructions CHANGE NO.: 1-2014 TO: County Directors of Social Services EFFECTIVE: January 31, 2014, make the following changes to the Child Placement and Payment System Manual. I. GENERAL

This change notice transmits revisions to the Child Placement and Payment System Manual. The changes are outlined below. II. SPECIFIC CHANGES COMPLETING THE DSS-5094 FORM Section III.K. ELIGIBILITY INFORMATION (fields 52 through 56) FIELD 55 Funding Source is revised to add 'IV-E' ADMIN ELIGIBLE' as a source to capture a child's IV-E eligibility and reimbursement status.

III. INSTRUCTIONS FOR MAINTENANCE OF THE CHILD PLACEMENT AND PAYMENT SYSTEM MANUAL

To maintain your current hard copy of the CPPS Manual:

1. Go to the DSS On-Line Manuals Change Notice website at

http://info.dhhs.state.nc.us/olm/manuals/dss/csm-80/chg/

2. Click on Change No. 1-2014

3. Click on the "CN 1-2014" and attachment links at the bottom of the page to print the

Change Notice and the attached documents.

4. Open the files using Adobe Acrobat Reader and print the pages. Insert or replace the

appropriate pages in your current hard copy as indicated below.

Note: Adobe Acrobat Reader may be downloaded for free at http://www.adobe.com/
