DSS ADMINISTRATIVE LETTER FAMILY SUPPORT AND CHILD WELFARE SERVICES FSCWS-08-07

TO: County Directors of Social Services

ATTENTION: Work First Administrators, Program Managers, and Supervisors

DATE: August 9, 2007

SUBJECT: USE OF MINIMUM WAGE FOR WORK EXPERIENCE

EFFECTIVE DATE: September 1, 2007

Effective January 1, 2007, the minimum wage in North Carolina was increased to $6.15 an hour.

According to the North Carolina Department of Labor, the minimum wage of North Carolina will increase over the next two years as indicated in the chart below.

Effective Date Minimum Wage

| | | | Jan.1,2007.— | $6.15 | | July 24,2008 | $6.55 | | July 24,2009 _—s $7.25 |

When determining hours for work experience, use the current minimum wage of $6.15 an hour rather than $5.15 an hour. A manual change will be forthcoming reflecting this change in Work First policy. This change is effective upon the receipt of this letter.

If you have questions around this issue, please contact your Work First Representative or Gretchen Williams at (919)-733-4622.
