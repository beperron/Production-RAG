CHANGE NOTICE FOR MANUAL 02-2008 DATE: January 9, 2008 MANUAL: Family Services, Volume I: Children's Services, Chapter IV-1201 Child Placement Services TO: County Directors of Social Services RE: Revised Manual EFFECTIVE: Immediately Family Services, Volume I: Children's Services, Chapter IV - 1201 Child Placement Services Appendix B, has been revised to include new policy on use of the Responsible Individuals List when completing the Kinship Care Initial Assessment and the Comprehensive Assessment for Guardianship. The new policy requires checking the Responsible Individuals List and county CPS records rather than the Central Registry. These revisions are all contained in Appendix B of the manual. Please ensure that staff responsible for the delivery of these services are made aware of this policy change. Should you have any questions, contact your Children's Program Representative or Thomas Smith of the Foster Care and Adoption Policy Team at (919) 733-9465 or at thomas.smith@ncmail.net..
