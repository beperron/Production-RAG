DATE: March 25, 2008 MANUAL: Family Services Manual, Volume 1: Children's Services, Chapter VI: Adoption Manual CHANGE NO: 01-2008

TO: County Directors of Social Services ATTENTION: CPS Program Managers and Supervisors CPS Social Workers RE: Confidential Intermediary Policy EFFECTIVE: April 1, 2008

I. BACKGROUND The North Carolina General Assembly in Session Law 2007-262; House Bill 445 passed N.C.G.S. 48-9-104 Release of Identifying information effective January 1, 2008. This legislation provides that an adoption agency licensed by the North Carolina Division of Social Services or a North Carolina county department of social services may agree to be a Confidential Intermediary (CI) for:

A. Biological Parent: by facilitating contact or the sharing of identifying information with

adult adoptees or adult lineal descendants of deceased adoptees with the written consent of the parties to the contact and the sharing of information.

B. Adult Adoptee: by facilitating contact or the sharing of identifying information with

biological parents with the written consent of the parties to the contact and the sharing of information.

C. Adult Lineal Descendant of deceased adoptee: by facilitating contact or the sharing

of identifying information with the biological parent of the deceased adoptee with the written consent of the parties to the contact and the sharing of information. Verification may be made via certified birth certificate, death or marriage certificate.

D. Adoptive Parent: by obtaining and sharing current non-identifying birth family health

information for a minor adoptee with the written consent of the parties to the contact and the sharing of non-identifying information. As a result of the legislation, Confidential Intermediary Policy was drafted by the Division of Social Services and recently approved by the NCACDSS. The approved Confidential Intermediary Policy is now being incorporated into Chapter VI: Adoption Services Manual.
