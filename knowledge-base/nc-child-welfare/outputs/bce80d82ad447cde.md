IMMEDIATELY I. BACKGROUND / CHANGE INTENT

On October 7, 2008, the Fostering Connections to Success and Increasing Adoptions Act of 2008 (Public Law 110-351) was signed into law. DSS Administrative Letter CWS-02-09 dated March 17, 2009 was developed to bring North Carolina in compliance with the provisions outlined in the federal legislation. One provision of the legislation is to ensure relatives are given opportunities to participate in the care and planning of children under the responsibility of state and local child welfare agencies. The attached policy to this letter updates the Family Services Manual with this portion of the legislation and expands upon the administrative letter to give practice guidance as it relates to relative notification. Additionally, three new sample forms were developed and added to the DSS on-line forms directory to assist county Departments to comply with the new legislation. Children Services Committee of the North Carolina Association of County Directors of Social Services approved these changes on August 12th, 2009. II. SIGNIFICANT CHANGES

Family Services Manual: Volume I; Chapter IV, Section 1201 on "Placement Decision Making" has been updated with a new policy section, "Relative Notification".

<!-- page: 2 -->
This policy instructs county Departments about the federal mandate to provide written and verbal notification to relatives of children who have been placed into the care and custody of the Department. Included within the new policy section are links to the suggested forms, Relative Letter (DSS- 5317), Relative Interest Form (DSS-5316), and Relative Search Information (DSS-5318) to assist county Departments with compliance of this legislation. Additionally, references and links to the new policy on relative notification have been added, where applicable, in Section 1201 and in Chapter VIII; Sections 1408 and 1412. Please ensure that all staff members who have child welfare responsibility are provided copies of this material or can access it via the NCDSS web site. The Division of Social Services on-line manual web address is: http://info.dhhs.state.nc.us/olm/manuals/default.aspx. III. INSTRUCTIONS:
