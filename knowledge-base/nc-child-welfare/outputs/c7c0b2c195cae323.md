NOTIFICATION OF POLICY MANUAL UPDATE

The Permanency Planning Manual has been updated and policy revised to incorporate the following required changes in response to House Bill 769 Session Law 2021-144:

• Addition of the Foster Parents' Bill of Rights throughout the manual

Additionally, the Permanency Planning Manual has been updated and policy revised to incorporate the below changes:

• Consistency throughout the policy manual regarding the protocol for Child and Family Team (CFT) meetings with clarifying guidance.

• Addition of a hyperlink to Appendix 3.5 Child Welfare Funding-Foster Care under the IV-E Eligibility Determination Process for Foster Care 18-21.

The above changes have been made in the Permanency Planning Manual. This manual can be located at https://policies.ncdhhs.gov/divisional/social-services/child-welfare/policy-manuals. Should you have questions regarding the Foster Parents' Bill of Rights, please contact Mary Torr at mary.torr@dhhs.nc.gov. All other questions regarding the policy changes should be directed to Margaret Knight at margaret.knight@dhhs.nc.gov.
