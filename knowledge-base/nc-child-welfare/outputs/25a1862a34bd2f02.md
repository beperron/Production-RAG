DATE: November 1, 2014 MANUAL: Child Placement and Payment System TO: County Directors of Child Welfare Agencies ATTENTION: Child Welfare Staff RE: Revision of the Child Placement and Payment System Manual EFFECTIVE: Immediately

I. BACKGROUND | CHANGE INTENT

The Child Placement and Payment System (CPPS) Manual has been updated. Almost every section of this manual was impacted, therefore it will be necessary for counties to discard their old CPPS Manual and begin using the updated version. Please note that the NC Division of Social Services (NCDSS) no longer indicates revisions by using red font and underlining. All font color and style will be the same throughout the manual.

II. SPECIFIC CHANGES INCLUDED

 Information regarding Reinstatement of Parental Rights (RPR).  Information regarding the Services Information System (SIS) identification merge.  Clarifying language regarding Contractual Agreement for Residential Services (CARS).  Clarifying language regarding Another Planner Permanent Living Arrangement (APPLA) and emancipation.  The format was changed to enhance readability and flow for the reader.  Contact information updates were made to both hyperlinks and the mailing address for the NCDSS.  Change Notice 01-2014 has been incorporated http://info.dhhs.state.nc.us/olm/manuals/dss/csm-80/chg/CPPS_CN_01- 2014.htm#P6_108

III. INSTRUCTIONS

Please note the above changes to the Child Placement and Payment System Manual (http://info.dhhs.state.nc.us/olm/manuals/dss/csm-80/man/) and begin using the revised manual immediately.

Questions related to the CPPS manual should be addressed to the Performance Management Section. They can be reached at 919-527-6260.
