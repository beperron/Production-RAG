MANUAL: Child Placement and Payment User's Manual - CPPS CHANGE NO.: 01-2010 TO: County Directors of Social Services EFFECTIVE: Upon receipt, please make the following changes to the Child Placement and Payment User's Manual. I. GENERAL

This change notice transmits revisions/corrections to the CPPS - Child Placement and Payment System User's Manual, Part III., Completing the DSS-5094 Form and Part VI., Completing the DSS-5095 Form. II. SPECIFIC CHANGES

The Child Placement and Payment System section is revised to specify the use of all zeros when a child's Social Security Number is unknown. These changes are outlined below.

A. Section III - Completing the DSS-5094 Form

Section III.B. - Field 7-Client's Social Security Number - is being revised to require entry of zeros (0) when the child does not have a Social Security Number. B. Section VI - Completing the DSS-5095 Form

Section VI.B. - Field 7-Client's Social Security Number - is being revised to require entry of zeros (0) when the child does not have a Social Security Number. III. INSTRUCTIONS FOR MAINTENANCE OF THE CHILD PLACEMENT AND PAYMENT USER'S MANUAL
