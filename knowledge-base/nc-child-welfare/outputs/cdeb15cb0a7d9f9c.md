# STATE CHILD FATALITY AND NEAR FATALITY REVIEW POLICY,

# PROTOCOL, AND GUIDANCE

## Purpose

The Child Abuse Prevention and Treatment Act (CAPTA) (Public Law 93-247) enacted in 1993 required that states have child fatality review panels and report specific data regarding the incidence of child maltreatment and child fatalities. Data is collected on both fatalities and near fatalities in order to examine policies, procedures, and practices of State and local agencies. In specific cases, evaluate the extent to which State and local child protection system agencies are effectively discharging their protection responsibilities in a review of child fatalities and near fatalities.

CAPTA legislation requires the State to document steps taken to track and prevent child maltreatment deaths by including gathering relevant information on the deaths from the appropriate sources in the State including entities such as State vital statistics department, child death review teams, law enforcement agencies, or offices of medical examiners, or coroners. Current CAPTA statutory requirements include provisions which allow for public disclosure of the findings or information about cases of child abuse or neglect that have resulted in a child fatality or near fatality.

The NCDHHS Division of Social Services (DSS) oversees local child welfare practices. When a child dies and there is current or prior involvement with child welfare services, NCDHHS DSS is responsible for ensuring that the local agency's practices comply with North Carolina statutes, rules, and policies, and that the agency has thoroughly assessed the safety of any surviving children.

## Definitions

Child Welfare Record Review: A review of practice in a case, conducted by NCDHHS DSS, when there a child fatality and a suspicion of abuse or neglect involved. This can include the fatality itself or history with the family when there is an open child welfare case, screened out reports, cases closed within one year, requested by a legislator, high profile/media involved case, and/or any case identified by NCDHHS DSS leadership. All Near Fatality and Fatality cases will be used to identify findings and recommendations for improving child welfare practices that may have avoided the threat of injury or fatality.

Child Fatality Record Review Team: NCDHHS DSS maintains a team that will receive all notifications regarding child fatalities and near fatalities for data collection and reporting purposes. This team will also conduct all Child Welfare Record Reviews on child welfare cases involving a child fatality and meeting the criteria for a review of practice in the case.

Local County Review Teams: Every county has a local team comprised of community professionals including the local county child welfare agency which meets a minimum of twice a year. The local team will review all child fatalities. The purpose of the local team review is to promote an understanding of the causes of these deaths, to identify deficiencies in the delivery of services to children and families, and to recommend and implement changes that will prevent future deaths.
