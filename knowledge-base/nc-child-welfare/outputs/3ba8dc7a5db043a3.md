I. PATH NC CHILD PLACEMENT AND PAYMENT INFORMATION

A. PURPOSE

Accurate reporting of payment and placement data for each child in PATH NC is essential. PATH NC will enable caseworkers to work more efficiently by reducing redundant data entry, increasing data accessibility, and enabling comprehensive case management. Caseworkers will see information icons beside data entry areas that provide additional information and indicate when something is a federal requirement. Additionally, PATH NC enables the following:

i. To collect information about all children who are in the legal custody of county departments of social services or who are in

foster care pursuant to a Voluntary Placement Agreement (VPA). ii. To collect sufficient information regarding expenditures made by county departments of social services for foster care

assistance payments and to generate reimbursement of state and/or federal funds. iii. To collect information regarding children who are placed in North Carolina under an Interstate Compact Agreement. iv. To collect information about children who are receiving adoption assistance and guardianship assistance payments.

v. To collect sufficient information to enable cash payments to be made to guardians and adoptive parents.

vi. To provide case management information about children and provide a mechanism for updating information about each

C. PARENTAL RIGHTS TERMINATION

Termination of Parental Rights (TPR) occurs either by court action or relinquishment. In PATH NC, the TPR date must be documented in the case plan or the legal record sections. Enter the TPR date according to the following guidance, regardless of whether the TPR occurs through a court order or voluntary relinquishment:

• The TPR date is the date recorded within the file-stamped court order (not the date of the stamp).

• Do not enter the TPR date until the appeal period has ended and the termination is final.

• Once the appeal window has closed, enter the date of the file-stamped TPR court order (not the date of the stamp).

- The TPR date is relevant only for social services purposes. For all other matters, the standard civil procedure rules apply. See § 7B-

1001(b): "Notice of appeal and notice to preserve the right to appeal shall be given in writing by a proper party as defined in G.S. 7B- 1002 and shall be made within 30 days after entry and service of the order in accordance with G.S. 1A-1, Rule 58" (N.C.G.S. § 7B-1001).

D. LIVING ARRANGEMENT

Living arrangement status of a child must be updated every time a placement ends, begins, or a provider ID changes. There must always be an open living arrangement while the county child welfare agency has custody/placement responsibility. Federal reporting regulations require that county child welfare agencies be able to track every move a child makes while in a county child welfare agency's custody/placement responsibility. Updates to placement changes or status should be completed within 3 working days of the move or status update. Counties should follow processes to make sure claims for reimbursement of payments are processed correctly and timely to mitigate any financial issues.

Placement Dates When entering the first placement episode, the "beginning date" is the first day that a child is in the placement (spends the night). The "last date" is the date the child moved from the placement. This is the last day that the child is in the placement. Counties can make updates in PATH NC for placement changes within the last 8 federal quarters. This would result in automatic adjustments in PATH NC.

Example: Child moves from one placement to another on the afternoon of July 10th. The ending date for first placement is July 9th, the last night of care in that home. The beginning date of the new placement is July 10th, the first night of care in that home.
