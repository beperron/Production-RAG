### CHANGE NOTICE

CWS-CN-04-22 MANUAL: PERMANENCY PLANNING MANUAL & CROSS FUNCTIONS CHANGE NO: CWS-CN-04-22 ATTENTION: CHILD WELFARE DIRECTORS, PROGRAM ADMINISTRATORS, MANAGERS, SUPERVISORS, AND SOCIAL WORKERS RE: REVISION TO POLICY EFFECTIVE: IMMEDIATELY INTENT: NOTIFICATION OF POLICY MANUAL UPDATES The Permanency Planning Manual has been revised and policy updated to incorporate the below changes:

• Addition of non-discrimination policy • Verification of Custody Letter protocol • Clarification regarding the 7-day initial health screening appointment (DSS-5206) The Cross Functions Manual has been revised and policy updated to incorporate the below changes:

• Addition of non-discrimination policy • Verification of Custody Letter protocol • Technical update to language for legal statues The following DSS forms are on the division's website:

• DSS-5133 • DSS-5133 instructions • DSS-5135 • DSS-5760 These manuals can be located at https://policies.ncdhhs.gov/divisional/social-services/childwelfare/policy-manuals. The DSS forms can be located at https://policies.ncdhhs.gov/divisional/social-services/forms. Should you have questions regarding the policy change, please contact Jessica Frisina at Jessica.Frisina@dhhs.nc.gov.
